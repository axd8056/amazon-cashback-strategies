## Understanding Cashback Tracking: What Works and What Doesn't

This is where many people lose cashback. The tracking mechanism that connects your purchase to TopCashback is fragile—a few specific actions can break it, and your cashback won't credit.

**What Breaks Amazon Cashback Tracking on TopCashback:**

1. **Clicking through and checking out on different devices.** If you click through TopCashback on your phone but finish the purchase on your laptop, the tracking cookie doesn't transfer. Keep your entire shopping session on one device and one browser.

2. **Using third-party coupon extensions at checkout.** Coupon-aggregator browser extensions inject their own affiliate cookie when they apply a discount code. This overwrites the TopCashback cookie, and the cashback gets attributed to them instead of TopCashback. The fix is simple: disable other coupon extensions on Amazon, or copy and paste promo codes manually from Amazon's official coupon page.

3. **Items in excluded categories.** Amazon gift cards (physical or digital), Kindle Unlimited subscriptions, Prime membership renewals, and some digital services don't earn cashback, regardless of tracking. These categories are off-limits by design—the commission structure doesn't support cashback on them.

4. **Ad-blockers stripping tracking parameters.** Aggressive ad-blockers can remove the affiliate tracking string from the URL when you click through. Pause your ad-blocker on TopCashback.com and Amazon.com during the click-through step.

5. **Cancelled or returned orders.** If you return an item during Amazon's standard return window (usually 30 days), the cashback voids at the Confirmed stage. Cashback only sticks if you keep the purchase.

**What Does Stack with TopCashback Cashback on Amazon:**

The good news is that cashback stacks with many other discounts and rewards. You can combine TopCashback cashback with Amazon promo codes, product-listing coupons, Subscribe & Save discounts, Prime member pricing, and credit card rewards on the same order.
